The Edge Stones story

A stone yard with a designer's eye.

Tucked between the hills of Erumbupalam, EDGE STONES began with a simple belief — building materials shouldn't be boring, and buying them shouldn't be complicated.

From Bangalore paving stones to polished granites, Mangalore roof tiles to evergreen artificial turf, everything on our yard is chosen by hand and priced honestly. No showroom games — just good stone, fair prices and a WhatsApp reply within minutes.
