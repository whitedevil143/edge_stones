Our story

Grown from the ground we sell.

EDGE STONES began where the road meets the hills — Erumbupalam, in the green heart of Idukki. We kept watching homeowners and builders drive hours to distant city showrooms, only to come home with stone chosen under tube lights and prices chosen at random.

So we built the alternative: a local yard that treats stone like craft, not cargo. Eight carefully chosen ranges, every batch inspected by hand, and one refreshingly simple rule — tell people the real price, the first time they ask.

Today we're the stop before the foundation stone: for courtyard Bangalore stones, cool Tandur verandas, granite floors, Mangalore-tiled roofs and gardens that look like they grew themselves.
